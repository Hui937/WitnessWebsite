Why Incarnation

Some may ask: Why did God work as the Spirit in the Age of Law, but has become flesh to carry out His work in the Age of Grace and the Age of Kingdom? Let's read a passage of Almighty Gods words to understand.

✨ Almighty God says: “God can save corrupt man from the influence of Satan, but this work cannot be directly accomplished by the Spirit of God; rather, it can only be done by the flesh God’s Spirit wears, by God’s incarnate flesh. This flesh is man and is also God, is a man possessed of normal humanity and also God possessed of full divinity. And so, even though this flesh is not the Spirit of God, and differs greatly from the Spirit, it is still the incarnate God Himself who saves man, who is the Spirit and also the flesh. No matter what He is called by, ultimately it is still God Himself who saves mankind. For the Spirit of God is indivisible from the flesh, and the work of the flesh is also the work of the Spirit of God; it is just that this work is not done using the identity of the Spirit, but is done using the identity of the flesh. Work that needs to be done directly by the Spirit does not require incarnation, and work that requires the flesh to do cannot be done directly by the Spirit, and can only be done by God incarnate. This is what is required for this work, and it is what is required by corrupt mankind. In the three stages of God’s work, only one stage was carried out directly by the Spirit, and the remaining two stages are carried out by the incarnate God, and not directly by the Spirit. The work of the Age of Law done by the Spirit did not involve changing the corrupt disposition of man, and neither did it bear any relation to man’s knowledge of God. The work of God’s flesh in the Age of Grace and the Age of Kingdom, however, involves man’s corrupt disposition and his knowledge of God, and is an important and crucial part of the work of salvation. Therefore, corrupt mankind is more in need of the salvation of the incarnate God, and is more in need of the direct work of the incarnate God. Mankind needs the incarnate God to shepherd him, support him, water him, feed him, judge and chastise him, and he needs more grace and greater redemption from the incarnate God. Only God in the flesh can be the confidant of man, the shepherd of man, the very present help of man, and all of this is the necessity of the incarnation both today and in times past.”
  • Whether it is God working in the spirit or the flesh, it is all God Himself.
  • In fact, the way God works in each age depends on the needs of humankind, the importance of the work, and its nature.
  • In the Age of Law, God worked as the Spirit because His work was to issue the laws and lead the newborn human race to live on the earth. It did not involve changing people’s life dispositions, the word He expressed were simple and few, so He worked as the Spirit.
  • In the Age of Grace, God redeemed all humankind through the work of crucificion on the cross. The Lord Jesus, in a sinless flesh, was crucified for mankind, serving as our eternal sin offering.

True and False Christ

Seeing that God has already returned and accomplished such great work on earth, we all feel very excited and thrilled. However, some brothers and sisters still have concerns in their hearts—that in the last days, false christs will appear to deceive people, so they dare not seek or investigate. Some even say, “If I hear any good news of the Lord’s return, I will simply not believe it.”

Brothers and sisters, is this the correct way to welcome the Lord’s return?

Did the Lord Jesus ever tell us to reject all news of His return? Absolutely not. He said, “At midnight there was a cry made, ‘Behold, the bridegroom is coming; go out to meet Him.’” We can see that rejecting people’s testimony that the Lord has come is certainly not in line with God’s will. No matter how many false christs appear, we can be certain that the Lord will surely come again. So if we flat-out reject all news of the Lord’s return, how will we ever welcome Him? Wouldn’t we also be denying the true Christ in the process? If you do not welcome the Lord, you cannot enter the kingdom of heaven and cannot be saved.

The correct approach is to learn how to differentiate between the true Christ and false christs—to understand the characteristics of false christs, so that we can have discernment against them and welcome the true Christ.

Let’s first read Almighty God’s words.

✨Almighty God says: “There are some who are possessed by evil spirits and cry out vociferously, ’I am God!’ Yet, in the end, they are revealed, for they are wrong in what they represent. They represent Satan, and the Holy Spirit pays them no heed. However highly you exalt yourself or however strongly you cry out, you are still a created being and one that belongs to Satan. I never cry out, ’I am God, I am the beloved Son of God!’ But the work I do is God’s work. Need I shout? There is no need for exaltation. God does His own work Himself and does not need man to accord Him a status or give Him an honorific title: His work represents His identity and status. Prior to His baptism, was not Jesus God Himself? Was He not the incarnate flesh of God? Surely it cannot be said that it was only after receiving witness that He became the only Son of God? Long before He began His work, was there not already a man by the name of Jesus? You are unable to bring forth new paths or to represent the Spirit. You cannot express the work of the Spirit or the words that He speaks. You are unable to do the work of God Himself, and that of the Spirit you are unable to do. The wisdom, wonder, and unfathomability of God, and the entirety of the disposition by which God chastises man—all of these are beyond your capacity to express. It would therefore be useless to try to claim to be God; you would have only the name and none of the substance. God Himself has come, but no one recognizes Him, yet He continues on in His work and does so in representation of the Spirit. Whether you call Him man or God, the Lord or Christ, or call Her sister, it does not matter. But the work He does is that of the Spirit and represents the work of God Himself. He does not care about the name by which man calls Him. Can that name determine His work? Regardless of what you call Him, as far as God is concerned, He is the incarnate flesh of the Spirit of God; He represents the Spirit and is approved by the Spirit. If you are unable to make way for a new age, or to bring the old to an end, or to usher in a new age, or to do new work, then you cannot be called God!”

The key difference between the true Christ and false christs is whether they can express the truth and possess divine essence. The words of the true Christ unveil mysteries, judge our corruptions, provide practical paths for practice, and bring confirmation to our spirit when we read them. False christs are wicked, embodiments of evil spirits, so they absolutely cannot express the truth. The words of false christs are human words and satanic philosophies that only mislead people. Whoever can express the truth is Christ; whoever cannot express the truth is a false Christ.

To gain greater discernment of false christs, let’s also understand how they deceive people.

The Lord Jesus said, 📖 “For there shall arise false christs, and false prophets, and shall show great signs and wonders; so that, if it were possible, they shall mislead the very elect.” (Matthew 24:24)
✨Almighty God says: “If, during the present day, there is to emerge a person who is able to display signs and wonders, cast out demons, heal the sick, and perform many miracles, and if this person claims that they are Jesus who has come, then this would be a counterfeit produced by evil spirits which imitate Jesus. Remember this! God does not repeat the same work. Jesus’ stage of work has already been completed, and God will never again undertake that stage of work. The work of God is irreconcilable with the notions of man; for example, the Old Testament foretold the coming of a Messiah, and the result of this prophecy was Jesus’ coming. This having already happened, it would be wrong for another Messiah to come again. Jesus has already come once, and it would be wrong if Jesus were to come again this time. There is one name for every age, and each name contains a characterization of that age. In the notions of man, God must always display signs and wonders, must always heal the sick and cast out demons, and must always be just like Jesus. Yet this time, God is not like that at all. If, during the last days, God still displayed signs and wonders, and still cast out demons and healed the sick—if He did exactly the same as Jesus—then God would be repeating the same work, and the work of Jesus would have no significance or value. Thus, God carries out one stage of work in every age. Once each stage of His work has been completed, it is soon imitated by evil spirits, and after Satan begins to follow on the heels of God, God changes to a different method. Once God has completed a stage of His work, it is imitated by evil spirits. You must be clear about this.”

False christs and false prophets primarily deceive God’s chosen people by performing signs and wonders. Why do they use signs and wonders? It is because they have no truth at all—they can only rely on performing signs and wonders to deceive people; this is all they know how to do. False christs have no ability or authority to usher in a new age or do new work, so they can only imitate the past work that the Lord Jesus has already done to mislead people. False christs cannot teach people about purification or overcoming their sinful nature. They are wicked and evil themselves and cannot teach what they do not know.

So, to tell true Christ apart from false christs, we must examine their essence, not their appearance. Let’s look at an example.

Brothers and sisters, which basket of flowers do you think is real—the one on the left or the one on the right? The left basket is real because of the bees. Real flowers have life; they give off fragrance and nectar, so bees are attracted to them. Fake flowers have the look but none of the substance.

In the same way, true Christ has the essence of God Himself. He can express the truth, do God’s own work, and possess God’s disposition. Anyone who claims to be Christ but does not have these characteristics is definitely a false Christ.

Signs and Wonders?

At this point, some people may ask, “The Lord Jesus could perform miracles and wonders at that time, and people could see that He had a divine essence. So, will Almighty God also perform miracles and wonders in the last days?”

Brothers and sisters, do you think Almighty God will also perform miracles and wonders in the last days? Let’s read Almighty God’s words to understand.

✨Almighty God says: “God’s work during the last days is practical work. During the age of Jesus, He did not come to make man perfect, but to redeem man, and so He displayed some miracles to make people follow Him. For He principally came to complete the work of crucifixion, and showing signs was not part of the work of His ministry. Such signs and wonders were work that was done in order to make His work effective; they were extra work, and did not represent the work of the entire age.”
✨Almighty God says: “In the last days, God principally uses the word to make man perfect. He does not use signs and wonders to oppress man, or convince man; this cannot make plain the power of God. If God only showed signs and wonders, then it would be impossible to make plain the reality of God, and thus impossible to make man perfect. God does not make man perfect by signs and wonders, but uses the word to water and shepherd man, after which is achieved the complete obedience of man and man’s knowledge of God. This is the aim of the work He does and the words He speaks. God does not use the method of showing signs and wonders to make man perfect—He uses words, and uses many different methods of work to make man perfect. Whether it be the refinement, dealing, pruning, or provision of words, God speaks from many different perspectives to make man perfect, and to give man a greater knowledge of the work, wisdom and wondrousness of God.”
  • In the Age of Grace, God’s purpose was to lead people to believe in Him. As long as they believed, they could repent and receive forgiveness. Since people didn’t know that God could become flesh, the Lord Jesus performed miracles and wonders to draw their attention and build their faith. These signs and wonders testified that the one true God had come to save humanity and confirmed Jesus’ identity as the Christ. However, the Lord Jesus didn’t perform miracles for their own sake. His goal was to lead people to seek His words and accept His work. Miracles such as healing the sick, giving sight to the blind, and raising the dead showed His authority and power, beyond human capability. Through these acts, many accepted His salvation, and His gospel became widely spread. We can see, signs and wonders supported God's work in the Age of the Grace but they were never the focus. How many times did the Lord Jesus turn water into wine? Once. How many times did He raise Lazarus from the dead? Once. Signs and wonders were a tool to draw people to God, but they cannot help people understand the truth, much less purify their sinful nature. In the last days, God is not trying to get people to believe in Him, God wants to purify our sinful nature and judge our corruptions. So, signs and wonders serve no purpose in this step of God's work, instead showing signs and wonders become detrimental to the work of judgment.
  • God can certainly perform miracles and wonders, but whether He performs them depends on the needs of His work and the needs of mankind. In this final stage of God’s work, if God were to perform some signs and wonders—say, feeding ten thousand people with one loaf and one fish—it would instantly shake the whole world, and all of humankind would come to believe in Almighty God. But then, how could those who love the truth be distinguished from those who do not, and true believers from false ones? It would be impossible to reveal people and sort them according to their kind. God’s judgment work in the last days is targeted only at those who can accept and love the truth. This time, God saves only those who recognize His voice and are willing to follow Him. As long as they see that what is expressed is the truth, they can accept it and follow. Saving these people does not require performing signs and wonders. Therefore, in this stage of work, God only expresses the truth and does not perform signs and wonders—this alone is enough to fully achieve His purpose of saving people.
  • From the pattern of God’s work, we can see that He gradually reduces the number of signs and wonders He performs. In the Age of Law, He descended on Mount Sinai with great glory and power, and the Israelites witnessed God’s majesty firsthand. In the Age of Grace, God became flesh for the first time and performed only some signs and miracles. In the work of judgment in the last days, God has completely stopped showing signs and miracles. We can see that God is turning mankind’s attention toward knowing and practicing the truth. It is like when parents lead and guide their children: in the beginning, parents help their children do everything—babies don’t even have to go to the bathroom themselves—but later, the children grow more independent and begin to take on more responsibilities. Likewise, God wants mankind to mature and grow up. Instead of relying on signs and wonders, people are called to use the truth to resolve their problems and overcome challenges. God’s goal is for His people to learn to live by His words and not by miracles.


When the Lord Jesus said, "My sheep hear My voice", it also means that in the last days, people will have to get to know the incarnate God through His words, and not signs and wonders.
